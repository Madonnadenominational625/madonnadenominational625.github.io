✨ What Is arca?

arca is a program that squeezes files together into a single package, called an archive. Think of it like a digital shoe box: instead of emailing twenty separate photos or documents, you put them all into one box, and the person receiving it only has to open one file.

Most people are familiar with ZIP files, which are the small folders with a zipper icon you sometimes see on your desktop. arca can create and open those, plus another common format called TAR. It can also make files drastically smaller using a modern compression method called Zstandard, and it can protect an archive with a password so that only the right person can open it.

arca is built in a programming language named Rust, and specifically in what developers call safe Rust. In plain terms, that means the software was written in a way that avoids a large category of common bugs that can cause programs to crash or mishandle your data. For you, that translates to a tool that feels steady and predictable while it handles your important files.

Because arca is open source, anyone can look at the code behind it. There is no hidden behaviour, no advertising, no tracking and no licence fee. You are simply getting a tool that does its job and stays out of your way.

🖱️ Your First Archive, Step by Step

Once arca is on your computer, the rest is straightforward. Here is a gentle walkthrough for making your very first archive on Windows.

  1. Open arca. Locate the arca application on your computer and open it. A desktop window will appear with the file area in the middle and buttons along the top.
  2. Pick the files you want to pack. Use the file browser inside the window to move to the folder that holds your documents, photos or spreadsheets. Select the items you want to include — hold Ctrl while clicking to choose more than one.
  3. Choose your archive format. Pick ZIP if you want the widest compatibility, since almost every computer on earth can open a ZIP file. Pick TAR if you are working with Linux or macOS tools and want a traditional package.
  4. Choose how much to compress. A light setting finishes quickly and keeps the archive close to its original size. A stronger setting takes a little longer but produces a smaller file. For most everyday jobs, the middle setting is a good balance.
  5. Name your archive and save it. Give the archive a clear name that tells you what is inside, such as Tax-Papers-2025, then choose where it should be saved. Click the create button and wait for the progress bar to finish.
  6. Check your work. Open the new archive in arca and confirm that every file you expected is inside it. This takes ten seconds and saves a lot of worry later.

To go the other way, open an existing archive in the same window, browse its contents, and choose to extract everything or just the files you need.

🔐 Protecting Files With a Password

When you create an archive, arca gives you the option to protect it with a password. This uses AES-256 encryption, which scrambles the contents so thoroughly that the file is unreadable to anyone without the correct password.

A few practical habits make this feature much safer:

  • Use a password with at least twelve characters, mixing letters, numbers and symbols.
  • Never reuse a password you already use for email or banking. Keep it separate.
  • Store the password somewhere you will not lose it. If you forget it, the archive cannot be recovered — that is the whole point of strong encryption.
  • Send the password to the recipient through a different channel than the archive itself, such as a phone call or a separate message.

Remember that encryption protects the contents of the archive, not the fact that you created it. Anyone who sees the file will still be able to tell that an archive exists and how large it is.

Is arca really free?

Will it work on my computer?

If you are running Windows 10 or Windows 11, yes. arca is also available for macOS and Linux, so the same archives you make on one machine will open fine on another.

Can I open arca archives with other programs?

ZIP and TAR are standard formats that almost every operating system understands out of the box. Anyone can open your ZIP archive on Windows, macOS or Linux without installing anything extra.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. All compression and encryption happens on your own computer. arca does not need an internet connection to work, and it does not upload your files.

What happens if I forget my password?

Because AES-256 encryption is genuinely strong, there is no way to recover the contents of a password-protected archive without the password. Keep a copy of important passwords in a safe place.

How do I update arca?

Return to the project page and download the newer version. Your archives and settings are files on your own computer and are not affected by updating the application.
